## ✅ Available now

### Editing
- As many picture and sound lanes as you need, with smooth playback and pinch-zoom on long projects
- Trim, split, move, crop, flip, rotate and place anything on the canvas
- Speed changes that keep the pitch, and reverse
- 46 transitions and animations
- Automatic proxies, so 4K edits smoothly, with the original full-quality frame shown when you stop
- Lock, hide or mute a whole lane in one tap

### Projects
- A projects screen with rename, duplicate and delete
- **Export a whole project** as one `.graden` file with all its media, and import it on another phone

### Colour
- Colour management matched to DaVinci Resolve 21 by measurement
- 28 camera and display curves and 20+ gamuts, from ARRI and Sony to Apple Log and DaVinci Wide Gamut
- Lift, Gamma, Gain and Offset wheels, contrast, temperature, tint, hue and colour boost
- Curves, LUTs, secondaries, a film look, noise reduction and deflicker
- Waveform, parade, vectorscope, histogram and CIE scopes
- HDR projects with HDR10 and HLG export

### Stabilisation
- A visual gyro that measures camera motion from the picture itself, no gyro data needed
- Rolling-shutter aware, with a 3D view of how the camera really moved

### Captions and text
- Auto captions made on your phone, in 99 languages, and nothing is uploaded
- Optional speaker and music detection
- 28 fonts including Hindi and Bengali, 14 in and out animations, and ready-made text styles

### Sound
- Per-clip volume, fades, pan, 3-band EQ, pitch and peak normalising
- Loudness normalising on export, for YouTube, Spotify, TV and more

### RAW and export
- MotionCam RAW (`.mcraw`) developed live from the sensor data
- H.264 and H.265 on your phone's hardware encoders, up to 8K, 10-bit where supported
- Rendered from your original files at full quality, in the background

## 🛠️ Being worked on

| Feature | What it means for you |
|---|---|
| **Cinema DOF** | Real lens blur after shooting, on the NPU on supported Snapdragon phones: automatic focus, tap or drag to change it, focus changes on the timeline, and lens looks |
| **Smooth slow motion** | Slow down any clip without stutter, using optical flow and AI that run on your phone |
| **Shadows, Highlights and Mid/Detail** | The last colour controls matched to Resolve |
| **Exporting captions as files** | SRT and VTT subtitles, with burned-in captions optional |
| **Caption style presets** | One-tap looks that combine font, animation and colour |
| **Landscape and tablets** | A proper wide layout |
| **RAW export** | Export straight from the sensor data, with stabilisation for RAW clips |

## 🧭 Planned

### Sound and music
- **Beat sync**: find the beats in a song and snap cuts to them
- **Audio noise reduction**: clean up wind, hum and background noise
- **Voice isolation**: pull a voice clear of a noisy room
- **Audio keyframes**: draw volume changes over time
- **Automatic ducking**: music dips by itself when someone speaks
- **Compressor and limiter**, for an even, punchy voice
- **Voice-over recording** right on the timeline
- **Audio-only tools**: detach sound from video, and edit the waveform
- **Sound effects and music library**

### Motion
- **Motion tracking**: follow a person or object, and pin text, stickers or blur to it
- **Keyframes for everything**: position, scale, rotation, opacity, grade
- **Speed ramps** with drawn curves
- **Masks and tracked power windows**, to grade or blur one part of the picture

### Compositing
- **Blend modes** and opacity for layers
- **Chroma key**, for green screen
- **AI background removal**, no green screen needed
- **Automatic face and licence-plate blur**

### AI helpers
- **Auto reframe**: turn 16:9 into 9:16 and keep the subject in shot
- **Silence and filler-word removal**, editing by the transcript
- **Shot matching**: make two clips look alike in one tap
- **AI upscaling and sharpening**

### Everyday editing
- More transitions, and templates you can reuse
- Markers, and groups of clips that move together
- Faster, better noise reduction for video
- Stickers and shapes

### Performance
- Support for more budget phones
- Faster exports, and lower battery use
- Hardware AV1 export where phones support it
